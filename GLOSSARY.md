# Cell Clicker

The words we use for the game's concepts, so the pitch, the code, the UI and AI tools all mean the same thing.

## Economy

**DNA**:
The game's only currency. Earned by tapping the Hive and from Organisms, spent on Upgrades and Organisms.
_Avoid_: DNA points, DNA strands, points, coins

**DNA/sec**:
The DNA earned per second without tapping, from Organisms and bonuses.
_Avoid_: income, generation rate, idle rate

**Tap Power**:
The DNA earned by one tap on the Hive.
_Avoid_: tap output, DNA per click

**Offline Earnings**:
DNA earned while the game is closed, at a reduced share of DNA/sec and up to a time cap.
_Avoid_: idle earnings, away income

## Things the player owns

**Hive**:
The thing the player taps to earn DNA. Its look changes with each Evolution Stage. Shown as "DNA Hive" in the UI.
_Avoid_: Cell Hive, cell, clicker, button

**Organism**:
A creature the player recruits with DNA that produces DNA/sec on its own. Each Evolution Stage unlocks new Organisms.
_Avoid_: unit, generator, worker, building

**Organism Level**:
How far an Organism has been leveled up with DNA. Higher levels produce more DNA/sec.
_Avoid_: rank, count, amount owned

**Milestone**:
An Organism Level (e.g. 10, 25, 50) that unlocks a new Upgrade when reached.
_Avoid_: threshold, checkpoint, tier

**Upgrade**:
A one-time purchase that permanently boosts Tap Power, an Organism or all DNA earned. Some are unlocked by Milestones.
_Avoid_: ability, perk, power-up

## Boosts

**Booster**:
A short boost the player can activate any time it's off cooldown.
_Avoid_: normal booster, power-up, ability

**Super-Booster**:
A strong timed boost limited per day (a few free daily, more by watching an ad) whose strength is set by how many times the player taps in the seconds after activating it.
_Avoid_: daily booster, mega boost, skill booster

## Progression

**Evolution Stage**:
One of the game's levels (Cell → Microbe → Bug). Reaching a stage's DNA/sec target evolves the Hive to the next stage. Progress is kept, not reset.
_Avoid_: level, tier, era, world

**Evolution Progress**:
How close the player is to the next Evolution Stage, shown once DNA/sec reaches 100.
_Avoid_: level progress, XP

**Achievement**:
A milestone (e.g. total DNA earned, best DNA/sec, Account linked) that grants a permanent Achievement Bonus.
_Avoid_: quest, trophy, badge

**Achievement Bonus**:
The total permanent boost to all DNA earned from every Achievement unlocked.
_Avoid_: achievement multiplier, generation multiplier

## Player

**Account**:
An optional online identity the player links to back up their progress in the cloud. The game is fully playable without one.
_Avoid_: login, profile, user
