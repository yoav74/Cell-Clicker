## Art and sound made by the team
No outside art or audio is used. These are drawn or generated in code:
- **DNA Hive and app icon:** `CellSpriteFactory` draws the hive (membrane, honeycomb, nucleus). **Cell Clicker > Create App Icon** puts it on the game's background as `Assets/Game/Art/Icons/App Icon.png`.
- **Icons, buttons, glow, rays, motes:** `ProceduralSprites`.
- **Tap, Buy and Click sounds:** `ProceduralSounds`.
